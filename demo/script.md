## A two-minute reset

Find a position that feels steady. You can sit or lie down — whatever lets your body rest.

Let your eyes close, or soften your gaze toward the floor. [pause 3]

> There is nothing to get right here.

<!-- page -->

## Breathe

Breathe in through your nose for a count of four. Hold it gently. Now let it go, slowly, through your mouth.

1. Notice your shoulders. Let them drop.
2. Notice your jaw. Let it soften.
3. Notice your hands. Let them be heavy.

[pause 4]

When you are ready, open your eyes. **Take this calm with you.**
